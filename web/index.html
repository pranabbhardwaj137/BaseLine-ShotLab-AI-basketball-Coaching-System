HOW IT WORKS

Evidence Before Advice

A scientific approach to player development built around verified video evidence.

1. Film or Stream

Connect your phone camera over Wi-Fi or upload practice footage. All decoding runs locally with zero cloud upload.

2. 3D Pose & Trajectory

Computer vision tracks knee dip depth, sequence lag, release velocity, and ball rim entry angle with mathematical precision.

3. Diagnostic Coaching

Receive comprehensive reports comparing your shot to pro baselines (Curry, Thompson, Allen) with targeted drill plans.

4. Interactive Drills

Practice dribbling drills with reference shorts videos and real-time browser motion rep counting.

KINEMATIC METRICS

Mathematical Form Analysis

Calibrated measurements capturing the kinetic chain from floor push-off to wrist snap.

Kinetic Chain Timing

Quantifies energy transfer sequencing: ankle push-off, knee extension peak velocity, and wrist release timing.

Release Velocity & Angle

Measures precise trajectory launch angle (optimal 45°–52°) and parabolic entry descent into the rim cylinder.

Pro Model Comparison

Benchmark every release against calibrated baseline curves of elite NBA shooting mechanics.

LOCAL & PRIVATE

Your Video Never Leaves Your Device

Engineered from the ground up for total privacy and low-latency offline analysis.

100% On-Device Processing

All video frames, pose landmark estimations, and ball trajectories are analyzed locally via SQLite and your local machine.

Zero Cloud Uploads

No video or audio is ever uploaded to external cloud servers. Your training stays completely confidential.

Instant Offline Coaching

Immediate rule-based diagnostic feedback and drill suggestions with zero internet dependency.

Before you film

Camera setup that measures well

  • Steady camera, side-on or at 45°
  • Head to feet and the ball stay in frame
  • Good light, no one crossing in front
  • One shooter per clip
Catch & shoot setup
