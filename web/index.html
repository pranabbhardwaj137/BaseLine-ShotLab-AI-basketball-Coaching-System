Before you film

Camera setup that measures well

  • Steady camera, side-on or at 45°
  • Head to feet and the ball stay in frame
  • Good light, no one crossing in front
  • One shooter per clip

Single-camera pose is an estimate. The app proposes attempts; you confirm them. It does not decide makes or misses for you.
